# Shas Master 0.1.0

Study Shas. Build your vocabulary. Make every daf your own.

Open the Mac download and drag the app to Applications. On Windows, run the installer.

Mac is signed and Apple notarized. Windows is unsigned and may show an unknown-publisher warning. Its separate project signature does not confer operating-system publisher approval. This release does not include an Intel Mac build.

Linux users [build from source](https://github.com/hatt-io/shasmaster/blob/main/docs/BUILD-LINUX.md). No Linux installer is supplied.

## Your saved work

Close the app before updating. Personal meanings, bookmarks and backups live outside the application and are retained when replacing it. Use Settings → Export to keep an additional copy of your work.
